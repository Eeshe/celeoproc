A power outage tracker for Discord.

Your server logs when the power goes out and comes back, and Celeoproc keeps the stats.

Add to Discord

How to use Celeoproc

  1. /postembed /postembed

    Posts the live status embed in the current channel. Members use its buttons to report when their power goes out and comes back.

  2. /activatealerts <embed_id> /activatealerts <embed_id>

    Turns on automatic alerts for the embed with this ID. Celeoproc reports outages and restorations on its own.

    Use /deactivatealerts <embed_id> to turn alerts off.

  3. /poststats /poststats

    Posts server totals, per-member stats and the outage graph. Without dates it shows the current month; optional start_date and end_date arguments pick another range.

Made for communities that lose power.
